Things you can click now show it: chat rows, sidebar rows, the small pills and icon buttons light up faintly under the pointer.

The small marks beside a chat's name say what they mean when you rest the pointer on them: an Agent chat, a NEAR Private AI chat or a Local Llama chat.

The Wallet window now shows how the chat in front is paid for: the wallet for Anon and Agent chats, your NEAR key for NEAR Private AI chats, and nothing for Local Llama chats.

A balance that hasn't been read yet shows a dash; rest the pointer on it to see why. The model list shows each price as in | out.
